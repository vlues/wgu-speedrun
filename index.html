Compare schools & degree plans

How much of each degree your credits can knock off, and what's left to pay for. Estimates only. Get a free unofficial evaluation from each school before you enroll.

JST default = Navy ABH3 with ~5 years: about 6 credits from boot camp, 3 from A-school, 15 from the ABH3 rating itself (almost all lower-division). Real JSTs range about 21–33. Check yours free at jst.doded.mil and type your real number in.

  • Why WGU wins on speed: TA pays at most $4,500 a year. Per-credit schools ($250/credit) get about 18 credits a year from that. WGU's flat term price lets you pass as many courses as you can for the same money.
  • Why UMGC wins on credit transfer: it's an official Sophia partner (your 3 courses map 1:1), takes up to 90 of 120 credits, and has on-base offices across Europe.
  • Why Embry-Riddle fits your rating: BS Aeronautics is built to absorb aviation training like yours. But Sophia isn't a confirmed partner there, so Sophia credit goes through a regular transcript review.
  • The catch with the most-transfer degrees: General Studies and Aeronautics take the most credit, but they say less "cloud engineer" on a resume than a cloud/IT degree does. Your certs make up a lot of that gap.

Realistic outcome if you work the board: TA + Pell refund + one or two mid-list wins = degree fully paid plus cash in pocket each term. And the biggest payout isn't listed — finishing two terms early beats every scholarship combined once the $85k+ job starts.
