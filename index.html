    Every degree plan, side by side

    Uses your checked Sophia courses and JST number. Green = knocked off, light blue = possible with more Sophia. "You pay" is after TA for the finish time you picked on My Plan. Tap a plan to make it yours.

    How to read this. "Knocked off" is what your Sophia courses and JST already clear. "Max" is how much you could clear by taking every Sophia course that counts. "You pay" is tuition left after TA ($4,500 per year) for the finish time you picked.

    WGU charges a flat price per 6-month term, so finishing fast is cheap. UMGC and Embry-Riddle charge $250 per credit, which TA covers fully, but TA only pays for about 18 credits a year.

    UMGC is an official Sophia partner and takes up to 90 transfer credits. Embry-Riddle isn't a Sophia partner, so its Sophia numbers are estimates. Get a free unofficial evaluation from any school before you pick.
