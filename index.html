    🎓 How many degrees can you stack?

    Realistic answer for about 3 years left in service: 2 degrees plus certificates, then a master's. All facts below checked on official school and Navy pages, Sept 2026.

    1
    Associate's from the US Naval Community College (USNCC) — FREE.
    Paid entirely by USNCC, not TA or GI Bill. Open to active-duty enlisted sailors. Programs run with UMGC (General Studies + Computer Studies, AI or Data Analytics), WGU (Cybersecurity, Data Analytics), Embry-Riddle and others. Every one includes 5 required Naval Studies courses (15 credits). Programs ↗
    2
    Bachelor's at UMGC — paid by TA.
    Your associate's rolls straight in (USNCC partners with UMGC). UMGC takes up to 90 transfer credits, so Sophia and JST stack on top. TA pays because it's a higher level than what you hold.
    +
    Certificates earned inside the bachelor's.
    Example: UMGC's 18-credit Cloud Computing & Networking certificate counts fully toward the Cybersecurity Technology bachelor's. A resume line halfway through, no extra classes.
    3
    Next: a master's, not a second bachelor's.
    Navy TA won't pay for a second degree at the same level. A second UMGC bachelor's also needs 30 new UMGC credits and 150 total. TA does pay for a master's, and your GI Bill is still untouched.

    Embry-Riddle route: associate's rolls into its bachelor's, and active-duty students can take their Embry-Riddle credits at any point instead of last. A USNCC → Embry-Riddle transfer agreement wasn't found, so ask Embry-Riddle.

    ⚓ USNCC + Sophia: the free associate's

    ⚠️
    Do Sophia BEFORE you start USNCC.
    USNCC doesn't allow taking classes at another college, or using TA, while you're enrolled. Finish your Sophia courses first, then send the transcript when you apply. You also need at least 6 months left in service when each class starts.

    🇺🇸🏠
    The smart Europe move: US-base jobs.
    Defense contractors in Germany can get you work status through the DoD's DOCPER/TESA process, so you need no German visa. DoD civilian (GS) jobs at Ramstein, Stuttgart, Italy or Spain are on USAJobs.gov. Overseas GS pay has no locality bump, but hires from the US can get a housing allowance (check current rates at aoprals.state.gov).
